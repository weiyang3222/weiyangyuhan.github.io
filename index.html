03 / ABOUT ME

A little about me.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in materials science and engineering at Westlake University. My research focuses on electrochemical control of functional oxides, with particular interest in ionic point defects, interfacial electrochemistry, and coupled transport phenomena.

I combine electrochemical measurements, thin-film materials synthesis, operando optical and structural probes, and quantitative modeling to study how materials respond under realistic conditions.
