RESEARCH / OVERVIEW

From electrochemical stimuli to material response.

My research connects ionic defects, surface reaction kinetics, bulk transport, and the evolving electronic and structural states of functional oxide thin films.

01 / MATERIAL SYSTEMS
FUNCTIONAL OXIDE IONICS

Controlling ionic defects in oxide thin films.

Electrochemical ion insertion provides an effective route to tune the properties of transition-metal oxides at room temperature. I investigate how proton incorporation and extraction couple to structural, electronic, and electrochemical responses.

02 / FUNDAMENTAL MECHANISMS
INTERFACE & BULK

Separating surface reactions from bulk diffusion.

Interfacial charge-transfer reactions and bulk ion transport are naturally coupled. Frequency-domain electrochemical perturbations and transport modeling provide opportunities to disentangle the characteristic response of each process under comparable electrochemical conditions.

EISTransport-line modelsChemical diffusionFrequency response
03 / EXPERIMENTAL METHODOLOGY
OPERANDO & DEPTH SELECTIVITY

Watching chemistry evolve in time and depth.

I combine electrochemical measurements with operando optical and X-ray methods. Time-, frequency-, and depth-dependent responses can reveal how chemical states and ionic distributions evolve across functional oxide films.
