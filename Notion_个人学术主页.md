## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Materials Science and Engineering at Westlake University, in a joint program with Zhejiang University, under the supervision of Prof. Qiyang Lu. My research focuses on electrochemical ion intercalation, defect chemistry, and ion transport in functional oxide thin films under room-temperature aqueous conditions. I combine electrochemical measurements with operando spectroscopy and diffraction to understand the relationship between defect chemistry, kinetics, and material properties.

## Research Interests

### Electrochemical Ion Intercalation
Investigating insertion and extraction of mobile ions in functional oxide thin films and the associated changes in defect chemistry and physical properties.

### Interface Kinetics & Chemical Diffusion
Quantification of chemical capacitance, interfacial electrochemical reactions, and chemical diffusion coefficients in oxide thin films.

### Operando Characterization
Combining electrochemistry with UV–vis, XRD, APXPS, Raman spectroscopy, and other multimodal techniques.
